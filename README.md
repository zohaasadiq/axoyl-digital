# Axoyl Digital — Official Multi-Page Website

High-end, bespoke multi-page Next.js website built for **Axoyl Digital** (Social Media Management & Creative Marketing Agency).

Designed with the luxury visual aesthetics of **Trifid Media** and **Roys Digital Media**, tailored precisely to Axoyl Digital's brand guidelines (`#520000` Maroon and `#D7C3B1` Warm Beige) with high-contrast accessibility.

---

## 💎 Key Features Implemented

1. **Brand Positioning**:
   - Explicitly positions Axoyl as **primarily a Social Media Management (SMM) agency** and **secondarily a full-funnel marketing & PR agency**.
   - Color harmony honoring the official brand kit: Rich Velvet Maroon (`#520000` / `#180202`), Warm Linen Beige (`#D7C3B1`), Crisp Ivory text (`#FAF7F4`), and high contrast ratios for readability.
   - Editorial typography pairing TAN Pearl-inspired luxury serif display with crisp `Inter` body text.

2. **The Client Display System (Trifid Media Style)**:
   - Modern pill badge: `Brands Collaborations`
   - Bold headline: `Brands that trust us.`
   - Seamless dual-direction infinite marquee ticker showcasing 25+ genuine partner brand logos extracted directly from Axoyl's portfolio.
   - Interactive Industry Performance Cards (*Hestia Harlow, Sarivaa Aesthetics, ER Organics, Kaked Bakery*).

3. **The Testimonial Display System (Trifid Media Style)**:
   - Split-screen editorial layout with live counter (`01 / 06`), circular Prev/Next buttons, vertical luxury gradient accent bar, decorative quotation icon, large editorial quote text, initials avatar badge, author designation, verified review tag, and 5-star rating pill.
   - All 6 client testimonials word-for-word from Page 20 (*Andrew Shortell, Maida, Ahmad Irfan, Sarah, David Lee, Emily*).

4. **Grand Founder Spotlight Card**:
   - **Esha** (*Founder & Creative Head*) commands prominent spotlight above the specialist team, featuring her executive creative philosophy, direct client endorsements, luxury portrait frame, and direct WhatsApp connect button.

5. **Conversion & Booking System**:
   - Interactive **1:1 Social Media Audit** modal with objective selector, industry dropdown, and handle input available across all pages.
   - Direct pre-filled WhatsApp link (`+92 349 2976505`) and direct email (`axoyldigital@gmail.com`).
